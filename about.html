About

Theory built from inside the problem, not applied to it from outside.

Root Studio Systems is the operating layer behind Liberation Logic, verification suppression, and cooperative economics research — the point where documentation becomes infrastructure, and infrastructure becomes something a community can actually hold.

This work runs on a resident learner method: theory derived from lived case immersion, not imported and applied after the fact. The frameworks published here — Liberation Logic's nested control systems, verification suppression's extension of the rational criminal model — were built from direct documentation, court filings, inspection records, and community organizing, not from a literature review at a remove from the people affected.

The through-line from research to build is not metaphorical. Solar Roots Co-op, Front Porch Economics, and CommonGround exist because the frameworks demanded a working system, not just a published argument. Root Studio Deployed exists to fund and extend that work — scoped technical capacity for other teams building in the same register.

Method

Resident learner — theory derived from lived immersion, not applied from outside it.

Lens

Afrofuturist and diasporic — liberated futures rooted in ancestral and community knowledge.

Practice

Cooperative economics, civic infrastructure, and documentary storytelling as one continuous body of work.

Reveal. Build. Liberate.
Asia Lakay Grady
